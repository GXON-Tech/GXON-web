ABOUT GXON

Built by Engineers,
Trusted by Farmers

Founded in 2014 in Henan — China's agricultural equipment heartland — GXON AGRO began with a simple mission: build grain dryers that work reliably in the tough conditions where farmers actually operate. Not lab-perfect machines, but field-ready equipment that starts on the first try and runs through harvest season without fail.

Over a decade later, we've grown from a small workshop into a 15,000 m² manufacturing facility producing four product lines: mobile batch dryers, fixed batch dryers, continuous drying towers, and complete grain handling systems. Every machine is designed in-house, manufactured on our own production lines, and tested before shipment.

TECHNOLOGY STRENGTH

Engineering That Makes a Difference

Core technologies developed in-house — the reason GXON dryers outperform generic alternatives.

Multi-Fuel Burner System

Patented burner design switches between diesel, LPG, natural gas, biomass, wood, and coal in under 30 minutes. Auto-calibrates combustion parameters for each fuel type.

Indirect Heat Exchange

Continuous towers use indirect heat exchangers that keep flue gas completely separate from grain — zero contamination, meeting food-grade and export standards.

PLC Automation

24/7 unattended operation with automatic moisture sensing, temperature control, and grain feed. Remote alarm capability lets one operator supervise multiple towers.

Heat Recovery Design

Continuous flow design recovers exhaust heat, delivering 8–15% fuel savings versus conventional batch dryers. Lower operating cost per ton of grain dried.

QUALITY CONTROL

Four-Stage QC Process

Every GXON dryer passes through four inspection checkpoints — from raw material to loaded truck.

Stage 01 — Inspection
Steel plate thickness, motor certifications, and burner component specs verified against standards before entering production
Stage 02 — Production
In-process QC at every workstation — welding seam inspection, dimensional checks, and assembly torque verification in real-time
Stage 03 — Testing
100% of units undergo full-load test — burner ignition, temperature stability, moisture sensor calibration, and emergency shutdown verified
Stage 04 — Shipment
Surface finish, bolt tightness, packaging integrity, spare parts kit, and documentation verified before container loading
