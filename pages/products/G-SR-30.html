FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about batch grain dryers.

What foundation is required?
A reinforced concrete pad sized to the dryer footprint, with anchor bolts per the installation drawing. Soil bearing capacity should be ≥150 kPa. GXON provides complete foundation drawings upon order confirmation.
Can the G-SR-30/32 really be used as a silo?
Yes. When drying is complete, the unit seals and functions as a storage silo, preserving grain quality for months with proper aeration. This dual-use design eliminates the need for a separate storage silo, saving both space and investment. (Applies to G-SR-30 and G-SR-32 models.)
How do I switch between fuel types?
The multi-fuel burner allows quick changeover between diesel, LPG, natural gas, biomass, wood, and coal. Typical switch time is under 30 minutes. The burner automatically adjusts combustion parameters for each fuel type.
What is the expected service life?
10–15 years with proper maintenance. The galvanized steel body resists corrosion in humid environments. Key wear parts (belts, bearings, burner components) are replaceable and available as spare parts.
Is installation included?
Installation supervision is included with purchase. The buyer provides crane, concrete pad, and local labor. Typical installation time is 2–3 days for standard models. GXON engineers guide the process via on-site visit or video call.
What after-sales support is available?
WhatsApp technical support, video-guided troubleshooting, spare parts express shipping, and optional annual on-site inspection service contract. First-year maintenance is free.
