FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about complete grain systems.

How is the system customized to my needs?
GXON engineers conduct a site assessment, analyze your grain types, daily volume, available fuels, and space constraints, then design a complete system specification. The process includes flow diagram design, equipment selection, and layout optimization. Customization typically takes 2–4 weeks.
What is the typical project timeline?
Design: 2–4 weeks. Manufacturing: 6–10 weeks. Installation & commissioning: 4–8 weeks. Total project duration: 3–5 months depending on system scope and site readiness. Civil works run parallel to manufacturing.
What modules are included?
Standard modules: grain reception pit, pre-cleaning (sieve + destoner), drying (batch or continuous), post-cooling, storage silos, and automated material handling (bucket elevators, chain conveyors, augers). Optional: weighing, bagging, dust collection, and fire suppression systems.
Is training provided?
Yes. GXON provides on-site operator training (3–5 days) and maintenance training (2–3 days) during commissioning. Training covers PLC operation, daily maintenance, troubleshooting, and safety procedures. Documentation in English provided. Refresher training available on request.
What about after-sales service?
First-year free maintenance included. Optional annual service contract with on-site inspection, remote monitoring via PLC, and guaranteed spare parts availability for 10 years. WhatsApp technical support and video-guided troubleshooting available worldwide.
Can the system be expanded later?
Yes. The modular design allows adding drying capacity, storage silos, or cleaning modules in phases as your operation grows. The PLC system is pre-configured for expansion. Typical expansion projects take 4–6 weeks without disrupting existing operations.
