FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the G-MR-20 PTO dual-power mobile grain dryer.

What power supply is required?
The G-MR-20 supports both 3-phase electric power and PTO tractor drive (90HP, 500 RPM). This dual-power design allows operation in remote areas without grid electricity.
How many people are needed for setup?
Two to three operators can deploy the dryer in approximately 45 minutes. The larger hopper and elevator require slightly more time than smaller models, but no crane is needed.
Do I need special transport permits?
No. The folded transport height is 4.5 meters, within standard truck dimension limits. The working height extends to 8.45 meters during operation.
What maintenance is required?
Daily: Clean dust filters and check fuel lines.
Weekly: Lubricate elevator chain and PTO driveline.
Annually: Full heating system and PTO gearbox inspection by certified technician.
What warranty is included?
12 months from delivery date, covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. Extended warranty and spare parts packages available.
Can it dry grain to storage moisture level?
Yes. Each pass reduces moisture by 3–5%. For paddy at 22% moisture, two passes typically achieve safe storage moisture of ≤14%. The larger batch size (20T) allows higher daily throughput per pass.
