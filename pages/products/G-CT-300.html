FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about continuous drying towers.

What civil engineering work is required?
A reinforced concrete foundation, supporting steel structure, and utility connections (power, fuel, dust collection). GXON provides complete engineering drawings and site specifications upon order confirmation. Soil bearing capacity ≥200 kPa recommended.
How does 24/7 unattended operation work?
Automated grain feed, moisture sensing, temperature control, and discharge. Operators monitor via PLC panel with remote alarm capability. The system automatically adjusts drying parameters and triggers alarms for out-of-range conditions. One operator can supervise multiple towers simultaneously.
How often does the heat exchanger need maintenance?
Inspection every 500 operating hours, deep cleaning every 2,000 hours. The indirect heat exchanger prevents flue gas contact with grain, ensuring zero contamination. Maintenance takes approximately 4–6 hours and can be scheduled during off-peak periods.
What is the installation timeline?
15–30 days depending on model size and site readiness. GXON sends engineers for installation supervision and commissioning. Civil works should be completed before equipment arrival. Commissioning includes performance testing and operator training.
What spare parts should be kept on hand?
Recommended spares: temperature sensors, belts, bearings, burner nozzles, and PLC backup battery. A starter spare parts kit is included with purchase. Critical spares are available for express shipping worldwide within 5–7 days.
Can the tower handle different grain types?
Yes. The continuous flow design handles paddy, maize, wheat, sorghum, and soybeans. Parameters are adjusted via the PLC for each grain type. Changeover between grain types takes approximately 30 minutes, including cleaning and parameter adjustment.
