THE CHALLENGE

Scale, Quality, and Non-Stop Operation

South America's commercial farms produce thousands of tons per day during harvest — requiring continuous drying that meets export quality standards without interruption.

Harvest Volume That Overwhelms Batch Systems

Brazilian and Argentine commercial farms harvest 1,000+ tons of soybean and maize per day during peak season. Batch dryers simply cannot keep pace. Only continuous-flow drying towers with 200+ TPD capacity can match combine harvester throughput without creating bottlenecks that delay harvest.

When drying cannot keep up, grain accumulates in trucks and temporary field storage — exposed to weather, pests, and quality degradation. Every hour of delay risks moisture reabsorption and reduces commercial value. The drying system must run continuously, day and night, throughout the harvest window.

Export Quality Demands Indirect Heat

Grain destined for international export must meet strict quality standards. Direct-heat dryers risk smoke contamination and uneven moisture, leading to rejected shipments. The G-CT-300 uses indirect heat exchange — flue gas never contacts grain — ensuring export-grade quality with uniform moisture distribution.

European, Chinese, and Japanese import markets enforce tight tolerances on moisture uniformity, foreign odors, and smoke residues. A single rejected shipment can erase a season's profit margin. Indirect heat eliminates the contamination risk at its source.

HOW IT WORKS

The Drying Process

From harvest truck to export-ready grain in five continuous automated steps.

1
Continuous Feed
Grain fed continuously from harvest trucks via intake pit and bucket elevator. No batch loading.
2
Multi-Zone Drying
Grain descends through 4 temperature zones. Each zone independently controlled for optimal moisture gradient.
3
Indirect Heating
Natural gas heats air via heat exchanger. Drying air never contacts combustion gas — export-grade purity.
4
Auto Discharge
Moisture sensors at discharge verify target moisture (13% soybean, 14% maize). Auto-adjusts residence time.
5
Storage/Export
Dried grain conveyed to storage silos or direct to export loading. Quality certificate generated automatically.

FAQ

Common Questions

Does the system meet international export standards?
Yes. The indirect heat exchange ensures zero smoke contamination, and multi-zone control achieves ±1% moisture uniformity. The system meets EU, Chinese, and Japanese import quality standards. Quality documentation is generated automatically.
Can it run on fuels other than natural gas?
Yes. The furnace supports natural gas, LPG, and diesel. Natural gas is recommended for lowest cost and cleanest operation, but LPG or diesel can be used where gas infrastructure is not available.
How often does the heat exchanger need cleaning?
The heat exchanger should be inspected monthly and cleaned quarterly. Automatic soot blowers reduce manual cleaning frequency. Annual professional inspection is recommended.
What level of automation is included?
Full PLC automation with remote monitoring via mobile app. The system auto-starts, auto-adjusts zone temperatures based on grain moisture, auto-discharges at target moisture, and auto-shuts down on alarms. One operator per shift supervises from a control room.
How long does installation take?
30-45 days for full installation including foundation, tower erection, electrical, and commissioning. GXON provides on-site installation supervision and operator training. Civil works (foundation) typically take 2-3 weeks before equipment installation.
What warranty is included?
12 months from commissioning. Covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. Extended warranty up to 24 months and annual maintenance contracts available. Heat exchanger carries a 5-year prorated warranty.
