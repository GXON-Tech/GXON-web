THE CHALLENGE

When Typhoons Meet Harvest

Southeast Asia's typhoon season (June–November) coincides with rice and maize harvest — storms damage standing crops and saturate grain, requiring emergency drying within hours.

Storms That Destroy Harvests in Hours

The Philippines, Vietnam, and Thailand experience 6+ typhoons per season. When a storm hits, standing crops are flattened and grain absorbs moisture rapidly. Harvested grain at 22–28% moisture must be dried within 24 hours or it begins to ferment. The challenge isn't just drying — it's deploying equipment fast enough, in areas where infrastructure may be damaged.

Fuel Supply Disrupted by Storms

After a typhoon, diesel supply chains are often disrupted. Roads are flooded, power lines are down. The dryer must operate on whatever fuel is available — diesel before the storm, biomass pellets or wood after, when diesel runs out or becomes unaffordable.

HOW IT WORKS

The Emergency Drying Process

From deployment to relocation in five fast steps — built for typhoon-season response.

1
Position
Tow the dryer to site on standard truck. No foundation needed — level ground is sufficient.
2
Deploy
Two operators unfold elevator and hopper in 30 minutes using hand tools. No crane.
3
Load
Grain loaded via elevator at 22–28% moisture. 5-ton batch capacity.
4
Dry
Dual-fuel burner heats air to 50–60°C. PLC controls cycle (3–4 hours per batch).
5
Move
Unload dry grain, fold elevator, tow to next site. Total relocation: 2 hours.

FAQ

Common Questions

What power supply is required?
3-phase electricity (50/60Hz), compatible with both grid and diesel generator. No transformer needed for standard agricultural installations.
How many people for setup?
Two operators deploy in 30 minutes. Foldable elevator requires no crane — only standard hand tools.
Do I need special transport permits?
No. Folded height is under 4.5m, within standard truck limits in most countries. No escort vehicle required.
How fast can I switch between diesel and biomass?
Approximately 30 minutes to swap burner nozzles and adjust air-fuel ratio. The switch can be done mid-season or even between batches.
Can it dry both rice and maize?
Yes. The PLC stores drying profiles for different grains. Switch between rice (50–55°C) and maize (55–60°C) via the control panel.
What warranty is included?
12 months from delivery. Covers manufacturing defects. Extended warranty up to 24 months and spare parts packages available.
